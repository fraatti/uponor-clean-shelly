Uponor Clean Card

The card below runs on power data recorded in Home Assistant from 8–18 October 2026 (Shelly EM Mini Gen4). Phases and messages were recomputed from that data with Shelly script v2.4. The simulated clock plays the data forward: scroll back on the timeline, press play, or pick a logbook entry to jump. Click the chemical value to set the amount in the tank. Process-tank and sedimentation levels are estimates from the phase history, not measurements. Card version 1.8.3.
